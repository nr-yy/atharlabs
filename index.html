Why “Athar”

Athar (أثر) is Arabic for a trace: the mark something leaves behind after it has passed. It’s the test we hold every app to. When you put it down, it should have left something good: an hour of real work done, a lesson finally understood.

Athar Labs is a one-person studio. Every app is designed, built and supported by the same person.
