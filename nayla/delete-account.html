Delete your Nayla account

Nayla, by Athar Labs

You can delete your Nayla account and all of its study data at any time. There are two ways.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nayla and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete for ever.

Your account and data are removed from the server straight away.

By email, if you can’t sign in

Email atharlabs.apps@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Nayla account”. We’ll delete the account and its data within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, and sign-in details.
  • All study data: subjects, lessons, study sessions and ratings, and deadlines.
  • Your membership status.

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, which must be kept for legal and accounting reasons. They don’t include any study data.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a membership. To stop being charged, cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data that only lives on your phone (your blocked-apps list, reminder settings and theme) is removed when you uninstall Nayla.
