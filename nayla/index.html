How it works

Four steps, every session.

  1. Pick a lesson

    Choose a subject and the lesson you’re working on.

  2. Start the timer

    Apps you chose are locked until the session ends.

  3. How well do you understand Genetics now?

    Not at allNot muchOkayWellVery well

    Say how it went

    One honest tap, from “Not at all” to “Very well”.

  4. Know what’s next

    Every lesson gets a score, and the shakiest one is a tap away.

Features

Everything it does, and why.

App blocking

Choose the apps that steal your evenings: Instagram, TikTok, games. While a session runs, opening one shows a “back to studying” screen instead. It stops the moment you finish.

Focus timer

Count up, or count down from a length you choose. It keeps going even if your phone restarts.

Lesson scores

Each lesson is Strong, Good, Okay, Shaky or Struggling, based on your own answers.

Needs practice

Nayla always knows the one lesson worth going back to, and starts it in one tap.

Weekly goal

Hours per week, eight weeks of history, and a recap every Monday. No streaks, so a rest day never wipes out your progress.

Deadlines

Exams, assignments and projects on a calendar. Each exam shows the lessons still shaky in that subject.

Statistics

Time and scores for every subject, and a trend that shows whether you’re studying more than last month.

Home screen widgets

This week’s progress, your to-do list, the next exam, and the lesson that needs practice, in colours you pick.

Gentle reminders

A daily nudge at the time and on the days you choose, and a second one only if you haven’t started.

Archive

Finished a course? Archive the subject. It leaves Home, and its hours stay in your statistics.

Works offline

Study anywhere. Everything syncs across your phones when you’re back online.

Questions

Frequently asked

When can I get Nayla?

Nayla is in testing now and coming soon to Google Play. This page will link to it as soon as it’s out.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Nayla is Android first, because app blocking works best there. An iPhone version is planned.

Does it cost anything?

Nayla has a membership, bought inside the app through Google Play. The price and any trial are shown before you pay, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

Can Nayla see what I do in other apps?

No. To block apps, Nayla checks which app is open and compares it with your own list. That’s all. It’s never stored or sent anywhere. The privacy policy explains exactly what is kept.

Does it need the internet?

Only to sign in the first time and to sync. Everything else works offline, and your changes upload when you’re back online.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. If you can’t sign in any more, here’s how to ask us instead.

Something isn’t working. Who do I tell?

Email atharlabs.apps@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model if you can.
