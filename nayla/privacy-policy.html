Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Nayla is a study app. It times your study sessions, asks how well you understood each lesson, shows your progress, reminds you to start, and can block distracting apps while you study. This policy explains exactly what Nayla stores, where it goes, and what it never collects. It is written to match what the app actually does.

Nayla is made by Athar Labs, an independent developer. For anything in this policy, email atharlabs.apps@gmail.com.

In short: your account and study data are stored so they can sync between your phones. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, and nothing about the other apps you use ever leaves your phone.

What Nayla stores on its server

Your account

  • Your email address, used to sign in and to recover your account.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only as a one-way encrypted form (a hash) by our sign-in provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile picture link that Google shares when you choose to sign in. Nayla never sees your Google password.
  • A display name, if you choose to set one.
  • Your membership status: whether your account has an active membership, and until when.

Your study data

  • Subjects you create: the name, icon and colour, and whether you archived it.
  • Lessons (topics) you add: their names.
  • Study sessions: the subject and lesson, when it started, how long it lasted, and how well you said you understood it.
  • Deadlines you add: the type (exam, assignment or project), name, description, due date, and whether you completed it.

That is the complete list. Nayla does not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, messages, or any identifier for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Some things are stored only on your device, in Nayla’s own storage, and are never uploaded:

  • The list of apps you chose to block.
  • Your reminder time and days, and your weekly goal.
  • Your theme, widget colours, and light or dark appearance.
  • A timer that is currently running.
  • What your home-screen widgets show.
  • Changes waiting to upload while you are offline.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

App blocking, and what it does not do

If you turn on app blocking, Nayla asks Android for two permissions:

  • Usage access (“see which app is open”), so it can notice when you open an app you chose to block.
  • Display over other apps, so it can show a “back to studying” screen on top of that app.

Nayla checks which app is in front only to compare it with your own list. That information is used immediately and then forgotten. It is never stored, never uploaded and never shared, and no record of which apps you use is created anywhere. Blocking runs only while a study session is active, and stops as soon as the session ends.

Notifications

With your permission, Nayla shows notifications: your daily study reminder, a second nudge if you haven’t started, an alert when a countdown ends, and reminders before your deadlines. These are scheduled by your phone itself. No notification content passes through any server.

How your data is protected

  • All communication with the server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules mean each account can only read and change its own data. The server enforces this, not the app.
  • Your membership status can only be changed by the server, after it checks with RevenueCat. The app on your phone cannot change it.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Deleting a subject deletes its study sessions on all your devices and on the server. A small marker of the deleted subject (its name, icon and colour) stays on the server so your other phones know it was deleted. It is removed when you delete your account.
  • Archiving a subject only hides it from Home. Nothing is deleted.
  • Deleting your account removes your account and all study data attached to it from the server. Do it in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, see how to ask us instead.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies, because payment records must be kept for legal and accounting reasons. They contain no study data.
  • Signing out removes your data from that phone, and keeps it on the server so you can sign in again.

Children

Nayla is meant for students aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and it will be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email atharlabs.apps@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
